A cloud computer that feels like paper.

Nova's brand is warm neutrals, one sunrise accent, and generous space. Nothing shouts. Everything breathes.

01 — Colour

The palette is a morning, not a screen.

Surfaces are warm off-white, the accent is a single ember, and dark mode is charcoal with a warm cast — never pure black. Every value is authored in OKLCH in tokens.css.

03 — Logo

One mark, two surfaces, no effects.

The mark is never recoloured, never glowed, never rotated. Keep 25% of its height clear on every side, and switch to the dark variant below oklch(0.35).

brand-icon-1024.png on cloud
brand-icon-1024.png on mist
brand-icon-dark-1024.png on night

Do

  • ✓Use the light mark on cloud, mist, or feather surfaces.
  • ✓Keep a clear space equal to 25% of the mark's height.
  • ✓Hold a minimum size of 24px; below that, use the favicon crop.

Don't

  • ✕Recolour the mark to ember or any status colour.
  • ✕Add glow, drop shadow, outline, stretch, or rotation.
  • ✕Place the mark on ember, sunrise, or a photograph.

05 — Structure

Space is the brand.

Rhythm runs on a 4px base. Radii are generous and pill-friendly. Shadows are wide, low-contrast, and warm — elevation should feel like light, not depth.

06 — Writing

Plain, short, second person.

Sentence case everywhere. Buttons are one to three words. Errors say what happened and what to do next, in one sentence. No exclamation marks, no emoji in product UI.

Instead ofOops! Something went wrong.

Instead ofClick Here To Get Started!
