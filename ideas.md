# Nova Cloud Computer — Ground-Truth Reference Direction

## Reference fidelity

This project is an original Nova-branded implementation inspired by the public Zo Computer homepage at `zo.computer`. The reference establishes the expected experience: a serene, editorial cloud-computing landing page with a slim navigation bar, monumental serif hero typography, gentle iridescent atmosphere, product storytelling led by workspace interfaces, community-space discovery, FAQ, and a dense utility footer. Fidelity to this page’s broad hierarchy, visual rhythm, soft material treatment, rounded dark controls, and calm confidence overrides the three-approaches brainstorm flow.

## Chosen approach: Iridescent Editorial Utility

**Design Movement.** The design combines late-modern editorial typography with the material warmth of a softly printed design annual. It intentionally avoids the conventional cold, blue SaaS-dashboard aesthetic.

**Core Principles.** The page should feel spacious rather than sparse, tactile rather than glossy, expressive rather than loud, and functional rather than decorative. Large moments of display type carry emotional ambition while miniature workspace UI elements establish product credibility. Every section changes cadence through an asymmetric pairing of an interface scene and concise text.

**Color Philosophy.** A warm eggshell canvas grounds the experience, while faint apricot, sea-glass, lavender, and butter-yellow blooms imply possibility at the perimeter. Ink and graphite are reserved for navigation, controls, and dense information so that actions are unmistakably readable. The ownable accent is **Sea-Glass Haze**, a gray-green-blue prism tint used sparingly in active states and translucent interface highlights.

**Layout Paradigm.** The hero reads as a calm, centered title plate with an offset workspace peeking up from the lower edge. The main body moves into roomy editorial pairs: a wide visual panel with a smaller, vertically centered narrative column. This is intentionally not a uniform card grid. The community section becomes a continuous gallery track rather than a grid of testimonials.

**Signature Elements.** A three-spoke Nova starburst mark appears in the header, workspace app switcher, and favicon. Display words use a softly offset chromatic shadow that makes key phrases look letterpress-embossed. Product screens have brushed-white bodies, hairline borders, and translucent color washes rather than dark browser mockup frames.

**Interaction Philosophy.** Controls respond with measured physicality: dark pills deepen slightly on hover, segmented options swap the content inside the workspace scene, carousel arrows move the discovery rail, and FAQ rows unfold inline. Navigation highlights are intentionally quiet. Every interaction remains keyboard accessible and announces a clear visible state.

**Animation.** Atmospheric color blooms drift slowly only when reduced motion is not requested. Product scenes rise a few pixels and settle on viewport entry. Tabs and controls transition within 160–220 milliseconds using a crisp ease-out curve. The display shadow has no continuous animation; it is a typographic material effect, not spectacle.

**Typography System.** `DM Serif Display` carries hero, section headlines, and selected quoted lines. `Manrope` supplies all navigational, product UI, button, and body copy. Display type uses tight optical spacing and generous line-height at smaller sizes; interface labels stay compact, tracking slightly positive, and avoid all-caps overload.

**Brand Essence.** Nova is a personal cloud computer for people who want an always-ready workspace without the usual software sprawl. Its personality is **thoughtful, capable, and quietly imaginative**.

**Brand Voice.** Headlines should be concise, roomy, and decisive; CTAs should promise the next concrete move, not generic momentum. Example lines: “Make room for work that matters.” and “Bring every loose end into orbit.”

**Wordmark & Logo.** The logo pairs a hand-cut-feeling three-ray Nova starburst with a high-contrast serif wordmark. The mark is used independently at large, legible scale as the site icon and in product UI.
